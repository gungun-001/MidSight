# 🎬 MedSight Demo Script (1-2 Minutes)

**Goal**: Demonstrate the core functionality of the MedSight platform from upload to diagnosis.

---

### **[Scene 1: Introduction - 0:00 - 0:15]**
**Visual**: Show the MedSight landing page with its premium glassmorphic design and the "Neural Inspector" button.
**Audio (Narrator)**: "Welcome to MedSight, an AI-powered diagnostic platform designed to bring dermatological insights to your fingertips. Today, we'll demonstrate how our MobileNetV2-based neural engine analyzes skin conditions in real-time."

### **[Scene 2: Uploading an Image - 0:15 - 0:40]**
**Visual**: Click on 'Analyze Skin', then click the upload area. Select a sample image (e.g., of Acne or Eczema).
**Audio (Narrator)**: "Our 'Neural Inspector' accepts high-resolution uploads or live camera captures. Once an image is provided, our pipeline begins: resizing the input to 224 by 224 pixels and normalizing the data for our deep learning model."

### **[Scene 3: Inference & Result - 0:40 - 1:10]**
**Visual**: Show the loading animation/stepper (Preprocessing → Model Inference → Generating Report). The result appears: "Diagnosis: Eczema", "Confidence: 89%", "Severity: Severe".
**Audio (Narrator)**: "In seconds, the model processes the image. Here, it has identified Eczema with high confidence. Notice the severity rating—our system automatically categorizes the condition as 'Severe' based on the model's certainty, helping prioritize urgent cases."

### **[Scene 4: AI Chatbot & Report - 1:10 - 1:40]**
**Visual**: Scroll down to the AI Chatbot. Type "How should I treat this?". Show the chatbot responding with context about the Eczema diagnosis. Then click 'Download PDF Report'.
**Audio (Narrator)**: "MedSight goes beyond just a label. Our integrated AI chatbot, powered by Llama 3.1, provides immediate educational context based on your specific results. You can also download a professional PDF report to share with your healthcare provider."

### **[Scene 5: Conclusion - 1:40 - 2:00]**
**Visual**: Show the Dashboard with updated stats and the history section.
**Audio (Narrator)**: "MedSight: Combining cutting-edge Computer Vision with a seamless user experience to make skin health more accessible. Thank you for watching."
